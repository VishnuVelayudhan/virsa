About VIRSA

The Vision Impairment Risk Stratification Algorithm (VIRSA) is a high-speed screening platform designed to solve this crisis. By leveraging advanced image processing and systematic scoring, VIRSA categorizes patients based on risk levels. Our mission is simple: streamline the screening process so that high-risk patients never have to wait for the care they deserve.
